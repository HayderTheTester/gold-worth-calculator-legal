Delete Your Account

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

Gold Worth Calculator does not require an account. An account is created only if you sign in with Google to use Backup. You can delete that account and its backup at any time. This page explains how, and what data is affected.

If you have never signed in, there is no account to delete. Your items are stored only on your device, and you can remove them in the App or by uninstalling the App.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Gold Worth Calculator app on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (gear icon) at the top of the home screen.
  3. Tap Backup.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Tap Delete to confirm. You may be asked to choose your Google account again to confirm that it is you.
Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your backup cannot be recovered after it is deleted.

What Data Is Deleted

Data Type Action
Account (name, email address, profile photo, and account identifier) Permanently deleted
Backup (gold items, display currency, and time of last backup) Permanently deleted
The App's access to your Google account Revoked
Gold items stored on your device Kept on your device. Remove them in the App or uninstall the App to delete them.

Signing out is not the same as deleting your account. Signing out stops further backups, but your existing backup remains until you delete your account.

Data Retention

After account deletion:

  • Your account and backup are deleted from our servers. Residual copies may remain in Google's backup systems for a limited period before they are permanently erased.
  • Crash reports and analytics data are not linked to your account. They are retained for up to 90 days and 14 months respectively, as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Data already processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's retention policies.

Alternative: Contact Us

If you are unable to use the App, you can request account deletion by emailing us at iamhaydara@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Please include the email address of the Google account you used to sign in. We will delete your account and backup within 30 days and confirm by email.
